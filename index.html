A HooBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds before ordering through a shopping agent. How to use your HooBuy spreadsheet shortlist

Save the original product identity

Open the CAFANS record and locate the original marketplace listing where available. Record the item ID, seller, selected color and size. Keep the original URL so a changed title does not erase the trail.

Confirm the item opens correctly

If you plan to use HooBuy, check whether its current interface accepts the original source URL. Match the item ID, pictures and variant. A CAFANS page is a directory record and may not be accepted as a marketplace URL.

Resolve missing details before paying

Ask about measurements, materials, availability and the applicable return terms. Confirm any payment, service and domestic delivery charges in the current checkout.

Review photographs of your item

When inspection photos are available, compare the actual color, quantity, visible condition and requested measurements. Ask for a clearer view if a photo does not answer your question.

Compare the complete parcel quote

Use the destination, packed dimensions and weight for your own order. Check what each shipping quote includes and which possible charges remain outside it.

Keep the record after ordering

Save confirmations, inspection notes and tracking details. Update the shortlist with what actually arrived, including fit observations, so future comparisons use your own evidence.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official HooBuy website?

No. This is an independent guide and product directory using CAFANS source records. It is not operated by HooBuy, and its main action buttons open CAFANS.

Are these real products from CAFANS?

These six records, titles, product links and source image URLs were retrieved from CAFANS. The selection is a snapshot reviewed on November 19, 2026. Current availability and specifications must be checked at the source.

Can I order every listed product through HooBuy?

Compatibility has not been confirmed. Obtain the original marketplace link from the product record, then check it in the agent’s current interface. Match the product identity and selected option before proceeding.

Why might the product price differ from this directory?

These are indicative USD figures shown by CAFANS at the time of review. Different variants, source price changes, currency conversion and additional charges can change the total.

Do QC photos prove authenticity?

No. Photos can help you inspect visible features, measurements and condition. They do not independently establish authenticity or confirm a material composition.

What should I do if a link is unavailable or shows another item?

Stop and compare the source item ID, seller and selected variant. Do not assume a replacement listing is equivalent. Mark the record unavailable in your own shortlist until you have confirmed the intended item.

Can I compare products without creating an account?

You can browse this selection, filter its products and use the planner without signing in here. External platforms apply their own account requirements.
